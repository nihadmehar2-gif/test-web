QR Code Scanning and Printing Tips

Maintain contrast

Use dark modules on a light background for the most reliable scanning. A contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 is recommended.

Print at the right size

A printed QR code should be at least 2 × 2 cm (0.8 in). Larger codes scan from farther away.

Test before publishing

Always test with multiple real devices before printing in volume.

Leave a quiet zone

Keep at least 4 modules of empty space around the QR code.

Avoid distortion

Don't stretch, rotate, or apply heavy stylization that breaks module alignment.

Use H error correction for logos

If you embed a logo, raise error correction to H to keep the code scannable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this QR code generator free?

Yes. All QR code generation, customization, and downloads are free.

Do QR codes expire?

QR codes generated here encode data directly and do not expire. However, if a code links to a URL, the destination page must remain available for the QR code to keep working.

Does my data leave my device?

QR codes are generated in your browser. The text and data you enter into the form are not transmitted to our server as part of the generation process. See the Privacy section for full details.

Can I add a logo to the center?

Yes. Upload a small PNG, JPG, or SVG (max 2 MB). We recommend error correction level H and testing the result with a real scanner.

What formats can I download?

PNG (raster), SVG (vector), and PDF. PNG works for most on-screen uses, SVG for print at any size, and PDF for documents.

Why won't my QR code scan?

Common causes: low contrast, logo too large, code printed too small, or damaged print. Increase contrast, use H error correction, reduce logo size, and test on multiple devices.

Is there a limit to how many QR codes I can create?

No daily limit is imposed by this tool. Very long input may exceed QR code capacity; in that case generation will fail with a clear message.

Privacy

Where generation happens. This QR code generator runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. The text and data you enter into the form are used to render the QR code locally on your device and are not transmitted to our server as part of the generation process.

Downloads. The PNG, SVG, and PDF files are produced on your device. They are never sent to a server.
